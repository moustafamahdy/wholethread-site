Wholethread was removed

The extension is uninstalled from this browser. If it did not seem to do anything, one of these is the usual reason.

If it didn't seem to do anything

  • Wholethread works in Microsoft Teams on the web in Chrome, at teams.microsoft.com and teams.live.com. It does not work in the Teams desktop app.
  • After installing, a Teams tab that was already open needs to be reloaded once before Wholethread appears.
  • Click the Wholethread button in the Chrome toolbar on a Teams tab to show the panel. If you do not see the button, open the puzzle-piece menu and pin Wholethread.

More help is on the support page.

Your data

Everything Wholethread did ran in your browser. There is no account, and nothing was sent to us or to any third party. The optional Fast export only read your chat from Microsoft Teams. See the privacy policy.
